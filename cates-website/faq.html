General

Is Cates a real estate brokerage or an auction house?
Both. We are licensed brokers and licensed auctioneers across eight states. We sell each asset the way it is best sold — traditional listing, online-only auction, or private treaty.
Where do you operate?
Tennessee, Kentucky, North Carolina, Georgia, Alabama, Mississippi, Virginia, and South Carolina. We also accept estate-services and downsizing engagements nationally; the home itself we list with our network where we are not directly licensed.
How long has Cates been in business?
Since 1942. Eighty-four years. The fourth generation is currently running the business.

For buyers

What does it cost to register and bid?
Registration is free. There is no fee to bid. If you win, a buyer's premium (typically 8–10%) is added to the high bid to determine the total purchase price.
What is "absolute" vs "with reserve"?
An absolute auction sells to the highest bid regardless of price. A reserve auction has a minimum the seller will accept; if bidding does not meet it, the property does not sell.
How does max-bid / proxy bidding work?
You enter the most you would be willing to pay. Our proxy only bids the next required increment on your behalf, up to your ceiling. Your max is private and is not visible to anyone — including the auctioneer.
What if my financing falls through?
All Cates auctions are non-contingent. We require pre-approved financing or proof of funds before activating your paddle. If you anticipate any uncertainty, call our office before registering.

For sellers

How long does an auction campaign run?
Real-estate auction campaigns are typically 30–45 days from listing to hammer. Estate and personal-property campaigns are typically 21–30 days.
What does Cates charge a seller?
For traditional brokerage: a standard commission, negotiable based on scope. For auctions: a nominal marketing budget (paid by seller) plus a buyer's premium (paid by buyer). Most auction sellers pay zero commission. Talk to a senior agent for an exact engagement letter.
Will my property be confidential?
Yes, by default we treat all conversations confidentially. For private-treaty sales, the asset is never publicly listed — it is marketed only to a curated buyer pool from our verified database.

Auctions

What is "soft close"?
Any bid placed in the final five minutes extends the auction's close by another five minutes. There is no time when a snipe can win — bidding always closes after a clean five-minute pause.
Are auctions held online only?
Most are. We retain on-site auctions for select estates. Online-only is what allows us to qualify bidders in advance and run the most data-rich auctions in the industry.
Can I phone-bid?
Yes. Our phone clerk relays your bids in real-time. Phone bidders see the same bid pressure data as online bidders.

Estate & downsizing

Do you sell foreclosures?
No. We do not sell foreclosures, fire sales, liquidations, or other distressed transactions. Our reputation is built on dignified estate sales and prepared marketing.
What is included in Cates Downsizing?
Eight steps: valuation, staging, sale, low-value disposition, next-home search, move management, unpacking, and two years of concierge afterward. The only call you make.

Technology & data

What data do you collect?
We collect first-party identity, behavioral, contextual, demographic, psychographic, and transactional data — strictly with consent and only to improve your experience and the seller's outcome. You can review, export, or delete your data from your account at any time.
Is the platform accessible?
Yes. We comply with WCAG 2.2 AA. The accessibility panel (lower-left button) lets you adjust theme, font size, contrast, and motion. We test with screen readers, keyboard-only navigation, and assistive switches.

Payments & closing

How do I pay if I win?
Earnest money (10% or $50K, whichever is greater for real estate) is wired within 24 hours. The balance is wired at closing, typically 30 days after hammer.
What about taxes and prorations?
Property taxes, HOA dues, and rents are prorated as of the closing date and detailed in the closing statement.
