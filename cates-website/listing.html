The estate

Designed by Tennessee architect Lila Ross in 2014, Sunset Bluff was conceived as a year-round lake house that performed equally well as a primary residence. The home steps down a gentle bluff to the water, with the principal living spaces oriented to capture both the eastern morning light over the lake and the long western sunsets that give the property its name.

Cantilevered porches, a 60-foot infinity edge pool, and a covered loggia with a wood-burning fireplace make the outdoor program every bit as considered as the interior. The kitchen and great room open as one continuous room across 78 linear feet of glass, while the four secondary bedrooms each carry their own ensuite and lake exposure.

Frequently asked — for this property

Is this property selling with a reserve price?
No. Sunset Bluff sells absolute, no reserve. The highest bid at the close — extension included — takes title.
Can I inspect the property before bidding?
Yes. We hold three open inspection days (May 5, 9, and 14, 10am – 4pm CT), and private inspections can be scheduled with the listing agent. We strongly encourage you to bring your own inspector.
What happens if my financing falls through?
All Cates auction sales are non-contingent. We require pre-approved financing or proof of funds before activating your paddle. If you anticipate any uncertainty, please call our office before registering.
How does the soft close work?
Any bid placed in the final five minutes extends the auction's close by another five. There is no time when a snipe can win — bidding always closes after a clean five-minute pause.
Are taxes and HOA dues current?
Yes. 2024 county taxes are paid in full. The property carries no HOA. All current assessments and prorations are detailed in the Property Information Package.
What is the buyer's premium and how is it calculated?
A 10% buyer's premium is added to the high bid to determine the total purchase price. Example: a high bid of $1,250,000 yields a total of $1,375,000.
