Valuation

Five-source AVM on the home; itemized appraisal on contents and high-value lots.

Step 2

Staging & cataloging

Professional staging of the home; photo cataloging of every contents lot.

Step 3

Sale

Online auction or traditional listing for the home; lotted auction for the contents.

Step 4

Donation, estate-clean, and ethical disposition of items that did not sell.

Move management

Insured movers, packing, transit, and timing coordination.

Unpacking

We unpack and place every item you chose to bring with you. You walk into a finished home.

Tax documentation, charitable receipts, and ongoing concierge for two years.
