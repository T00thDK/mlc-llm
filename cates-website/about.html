The four generations

A timeline of 84 years.

1942

First Generation — Great-Grandfather

R. T. Cates calls his first tobacco auction in Cookeville, Tennessee. The voice and the rhythm — the chant, the ring man's discipline — are the foundation that still informs every Cates event eight decades later.

1968

Second Generation — Grandfather

R. T. Cates Jr. brings the gavel to the front lawn. Estate sales, personal property, and the on-site auction tradition — which we still honor — become the company's signature. By 1980 the second generation is selling more than 200 estates a year.

1994

Third Generation — Father

Robert Cates III takes the company from personal property into real estate at scale: residential, land & farms, lakefront, luxury, hotels, and commercial. He earns the firm's first national auctioneer awards and seeds the brokerage practice that today operates across eight states.

2018

Fourth Generation — The Brothers

Carter Cates joins the firm, bringing a background in advertising technology, data, ad-tech, and entrepreneurial venture creation. Jordan Cates joins shortly after, bridging marketing and sales as Chief Revenue Officer. Together they rebuild Cates as a data-first auction house: first-party data, deep-learner price discovery, cross-device identity, and a war-room console second to none.

Our philosophy

What we believe about selling.

A property — a home, a farm, an estate's contents — has to be sold the way it actually wants to be sold. Some assets perform best in the certainty of a time-definite auction; others want a quiet, traditional listing and a long handshake.

We are licensed brokers and licensed auctioneers. That is rare on its own. What is rarer still is a firm that is unwilling to push you toward the path that is more profitable for them. Cates was built on telling sellers the truth — even when the truth costs us a deal — and on giving buyers the most credible, data-rich diligence package in our industry.

Our approach has been called "the best of traditional brokerage paired with the most sophisticated auction technology in America." We will keep earning that.
